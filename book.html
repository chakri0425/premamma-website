What the book holds

This is Premamma’s life, told by Premamma: a childhood in Parumanchala in a family pushed to the margins; the fight to keep studying when girls were kept at home; a working life spent bringing other children into classrooms; the grief of losing a child and the determination that carried her other three to become an IPS officer, a professor and a doctor.

It is, above all, a book about what education can do — not for one person, but for a family, and for everyone that family touches.
