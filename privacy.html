How we use your details

This website belongs to the family of Premamma. When you send a review or a message, we store what you type in the form together with the date, your IP address and your browser type.

Why: reviews are published only after the family reads them, and the IP address helps us spot and block spam or abuse. Messages are used only to reply to you.

What is public: for approved reviews, only your name, city, rating, review and date are shown. Your email, IP address and messages are never published or shared.

Your choice: to have your review or message removed, write to rspremamma@gmail.com or use the contact page, and we will delete it.
